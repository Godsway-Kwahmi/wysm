When You See Me

Short story · 2026

Adanna is a young woman working as a nanny in Ghana with a dream of attending university. When she meets and begins dating James, he seems like the person that will help her make this dream a reality. But even as James promises her a bright future, there are signs that his help comes at a steep price. Adanna soon finds herself having to make difficult decisions about their relationship and needing the help of the people around her to withstand the backlash.

Published by the When You See Me project, 2026.
