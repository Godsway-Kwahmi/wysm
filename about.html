About The Project

Gender-based violence prevention and community responses in Ghana

Research Framework & Narrative Methodology

Migration is a significant risk factor to experiencing gender-based violence (GBV), and women who migrate without their relatives often lack support to prevent or escape violence[cite: 1].

We employ narrative methodologies to study the help- and justice-seeking behaviors of female Nigerian immigrants in Ghana facing intimate partner violence (IPV) and non-partner sexual violence[cite: 1].

Applied Theatre & Comics

Our team converts research insights into educational illustrated stories and interactive applied theatre performances to raise awareness across host communities[cite: 1].

Traditional Leaders & Queenmothers

We collaborate closely with female traditional leaders ('queenmothers') to establish institutional and informal support channels for survivors[cite: 1].
